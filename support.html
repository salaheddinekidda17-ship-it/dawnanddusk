Support

Questions, or something not working? Write to salaheddine.kidda@outlook.com. We read every message.

Common questions

My product isn't found when I search or scan.

The app looks products up in Open Beauty Facts, a free list kept by volunteers, so it does not have everything. Tap Add it myself and type the name. That works without the internet and takes a few seconds.

How do I move everything to a new iPhone?

If you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, your routines come with it. You can also go to Settings, Your data, Save a backup file, and on the new iPhone choose Restore from a backup file.

I bought Plus but it isn't unlocked.

Open Settings, Dawn & Dusk Plus, and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. A purchase waiting for approval (Ask to Buy) unlocks by itself once approved.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders don't appear.

Check that notifications are allowed for Dawn & Dusk in the iPhone's Settings app, and that a Focus is not hiding them. A reminder arrives only on days when that routine has something to do.

The app says it couldn't open my saved routines.

Nothing has been deleted. Tap Try again first. If that does not help, you can go back to a recent copy, or send us the file from that screen and we will try to recover it.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, Your data, Erase everything removes it all from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. We hold no copy.
