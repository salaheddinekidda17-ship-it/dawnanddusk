Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. This policy covers the Dawn & Dusk app for iPhone.

The short version

  • There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your products, routines, journal notes and photos are saved on your iPhone. We do not receive them.
  • The only thing the app sends out is a product search or a barcode number, to a public product list, when you ask for one.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter: your name if you give one, your products, routines, the record of routines you finished, journal notes, how you said your skin felt, photos, reminder times and settings. The app has no server of its own and we cannot see this information.

What leaves your iPhone, and when

Product search and barcode lookup. When you search for a product or look up a barcode, the words you typed or the barcode number are sent to Open Beauty Facts, a non-profit, open product database, so that it can answer. As with any website, its servers also receive your internet (IP) address and the name and version of the app. Product pictures shown from that list are loaded from its servers. Nothing else about you is sent: no name, no routine, no notes, no photos. The camera picture used for scanning never leaves your iPhone. If you add products by typing them in yourself, nothing is sent at all. Open Beauty Facts' own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Purchases. If you buy Dawn & Dusk Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple may share with us totals of sales that do not identify you.

Support. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and use it only to answer you.

Sharing your journal

If you share your journal as a PDF, the file is made on your iPhone and goes only where you send it, for example to Files or to an email you write yourself.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, that backup includes the app's data, under Apple's terms. A backup file that you save from the app is stored wherever you put it; it is not password-protected, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Reminders, camera and photos

Reminders are created on your iPhone and show no product names or notes. The app uses the camera only when you ask it to scan a barcode or take a journal photo, and receives a photo from your library only when you pick it yourself. Photos are never retouched or uploaded.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. Apple may provide us with general, non-identifying statistics such as the number of downloads and crashes, if you have agreed to share them with developers in your iPhone's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from them.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings, Your data, Erase everything. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete.

Not medical advice

Dawn & Dusk helps you organise your own routine. It does not assess skin, judge ingredients or recommend products, and it is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, dermatologist or pharmacist.

Changes and contact
